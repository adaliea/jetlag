## Pairs and timing

- The __HIDING_MINUTES__-minute hiding period is a local extension of the Small game. Keep the printed Small values for card effects, Move, answer deadlines, and other timers.
- Each pair stays together, shares one hiding zone and one hider hand, and takes one hiding turn at a time.
- The other pair or pairs seek together, sharing their questions and decisions.
- Randomize the pair order before the first round. Aim to give every pair one hiding turn.
- Later rounds start at the previous hiding spot, following your rulebook's preparation period.
- A suggested play window is 10 a.m.–6 p.m. Eastern, with food and rest breaks between rounds. This is not a chosen start time or a promise that every round will finish.
- With three pairs, allow close to the full eight hours. Agree a hard end time and unfinished-round scoring before starting. No custom round cap has been selected.

## Map and stop centers

- The red border follows mapped roads and their junctions, covering downtown, University Park, the Waupelani corridor, and Martin Street/Vairo. The expanded draft reaches Scott, Shingletown, and West Branch Roads to the south and I-99 / US 322 to the northwest. Tap a red section to see its road name.
- Starting at Blue Course Drive and West Whitehall Road, trace this loop: **__BOUNDARY_STREETS__**, then back to Blue Course Drive.
- The mapped street centerline defines the edge; use lawful pedestrian routes on the game side. A boundary road or interchange is not a hiding spot.
- I-99 and its interchange ramps define part of the edge only; they are not walking routes. The border does not require anyone to travel around its perimeter.
- Only a blue marker for the selected day may center a hiding zone. Gray markers are open boarding stops whose full circles cross the edge; use them for transportation only.
- Every available stop meeting the map criteria is included for its day. Nearby and opposite-direction stops count separately at their exact coordinates; there is no handpicked subset or center-count cap.
- Every blue marker's full 400 m circle fits inside the road border. A center needs scheduled boarding service during the reference window for that day and must not have a recorded closure. It does not need service on the other weekend day.
- A stop can be excluded because its center or full circle is outside the border, it has no scheduled boarding during that day's reference window, or it has a documented closure. Search its name or ID to see the reason. Download the daily center list or exclusions CSV from the map.
- Long waits are shown in the stop details, rather than used as an automatic exclusion. Scheduled service is not a guarantee of availability at every moment of a round.
- The comparison toggle shows the previous 44-center border in purple dashes, the added area in green, and the three restored zones. Those comparison markings do not define the current game edge; use the red border.
- A circle being inside the border does not make every place inside it accessible.
- For local questions about the hider's station, use the named blue center, its stop ID, and the routes shown for the chosen day. Where a question has different wording, follow your rulebook.
- Use the same map version and the same Saturday/Sunday setting on all phones throughout the game.

## Geographic divisions

The division layer shows the actual **State College borough, College township,

Pennsylvania is the state and Centre County is the county. Use the mapped

a player's location, apply its printed wording rather than substituting the

## Weekend transportation

- Saturday has __SATURDAY_COUNT__ centers and Sunday has __SUNDAY_COUNT__. Changing the day changes the blue centers, gray boarding stops, route lines, search status, and downloadable center list.
- The reference schedules are October 3 and 4, 2026, between 10 a.m. and 6 p.m. Eastern. Select a real play date before relying on them.
- Full service does not mean the same service every day. Home-football Saturdays can use different routes and stops; this map does not model game-day detours.
- Check CATA alerts for the actual date. The recorded closures include stops 161, 471, 2004, 384, and 718. A stopped or relocated service can require removing a center or rebuilding the map before play.
- Buses may briefly cross the border while you remain aboard. Do not disembark outside it or use an outside location to answer a question.
- Use fixed-route CATA buses, including the campus loops. CATAGO!, reserved rides, event shuttles, taxis, private cars, bikes, scooters, and rideshare are outside this proposed game network.
- Bus waiting time counts. A center need not be reachable from every possible round start during the hiding period.
- Use the card and rulebook definitions for transit and the end game. Being at a bus stop is not being aboard a bus.

## Maps and location sharing

Agree one mapping app for directions, category searches, measurements, and
screenshots before play. Use this companion for the game border, blue centers,
municipal boundaries, and hiding circles. The location button shows only your
own device location; it does not share it.

Set up seeker location sharing so the hiding pair can see the seeker group.
The hiding pair must not expose its live location to seekers. Choose an app
that works for everyone; Find My is an option if all pairs can use it.
Follow your printed rules on Street View and other research restrictions.

## Access and hiding spots

__HIDING_POLICY__

- Follow the printed rules for proximity to mapped paths and roads, visibility, and being found.
- Suitable indoor areas include student-accessible lobbies, lounges, student-union common areas, and library public/common areas, where ordinary use and opening hours allow it.
- Exclude classrooms and lecture halls (even when empty), labs, offices, bathrooms, private residence rooms and restricted residential areas, service areas, restricted athletics spaces, and rooms reserved for a private activity.
- Access must be available to every player under the same ordinary student-access conditions. Do not use department-, course-, staff-, or resident-only privileges, tailgate through a locked door, or rely on someone else letting you in.
- Keep corridors, entrances, and stairs clear. Do not disrupt classes, studying, staff work, or other activities.
- Do not enter private homes, construction sites, fenced areas, rail infrastructure, or vehicle-only roads. Use legal pedestrian crossings and routes.
- A hotel or apartment name in a stop label identifies the stop; it does not authorize entry to the property.
- Check the final spot is open and accessible for the whole round. Leave immediately if access closes or staff ask you to leave, and pause to resolve the round fairly.
- Pause for a player feeling unsafe or an actual disruption. A blue marker is not an assurance that a location has been inspected in person.

## Before playing

- Confirm the date, Saturday/Sunday mode, and CATA service alerts.
- Review the red border and the blue centers together; remove any center the group cannot use comfortably.
- Confirm the __HIDING_MINUTES__-minute hiding period, student-access common-area exception, and the remaining Small settings in your printed edition.
- Confirm the pair count, rotation, start time, hard end time, and unfinished-round handling.
- Agree the mapping app and test seeker location sharing without exposing the hiders.
- Bring the cards, rulebooks, dice, charged phones, water, and a way to pay CATA fares.
